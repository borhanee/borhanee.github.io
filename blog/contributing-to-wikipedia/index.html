Contributing to Wikipedia

Published: August 1, 2026 2 min read Borhan Uddin

I have always been interested in how knowledge is documented, organized, and shared. For me, Wikipedia is one of the most meaningful platforms to contribute to because it makes knowledge freely accessible to everyone.

My contribution to Wikipedia is not limited to writing new articles. I also work on improving existing articles, adding reliable sources, correcting information, and documenting subjects that may not receive enough attention online.

One thing Wikipedia has taught me is the importance of reliable sources. Before adding information, I try to verify where it comes from and whether it can be properly cited. This has also made me more careful about how I evaluate information outside Wikipedia.

I particularly value contributions to local and regional topics. Many places, people, historical events, and cultural subjects are still poorly documented online. Contributing reliable information about them helps preserve knowledge and makes it easier for others to discover.

What I like most about Wikipedia is its collaborative nature. No single person owns an article. People with different backgrounds work together to improve the same body of knowledge.

For me, contributing to Wikipedia is a simple way to give something back to the wider community. A small edit may take only a few minutes, but it can remain useful to readers for years.
