Structural Design of a Five-Story Residential Building

This project presents the complete structural design and documentation for a five-story reinforced concrete residential building. It covers the engineering workflow from structural planning and detailed design calculations to construction drawings and quantity estimation.

The project includes the structural design of slabs, balcony slabs, beams, columns, footings, stairs, and a septic tank using standard reinforced concrete design principles. Detailed engineering drawings and calculation sheets were prepared to ensure structural safety, serviceability, and compliance with design requirements.

In addition to structural analysis, the project includes quantity estimation and cost calculations, providing practical experience in construction planning and project documentation. This case study demonstrates the integration of structural engineering, drafting, and estimation into a complete residential building design project.
